About this policy

This draft privacy policy describes how Senyum Rihlat Pte Ltd proposes to handle personal information in connection with travel enquiries and bookings. It is prepared for client review before the production website launches.

The enquiry form on this design preview is a demonstration and does not send a booking or enquiry. If you contact us through WhatsApp, email or telephone, the information you choose to share is received through that service.

Information you share

An enquiry may include your name, telephone number, email address, destination, travel type, preferred dates, number of travellers, estimated budget and additional requirements.

When arranging a booking, we may also need travellers’ names, passport or visa details, and information needed by the relevant travel providers. Please provide sensitive travel documents only through a channel agreed with our team. If you supply information about other travellers, ensure you have their permission.

How information is used

We use information relevant to your request to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, arrange travel, communicate booking updates and support you before and during your journey.

Our booking terms provide for sharing personal information with relevant partners, suppliers and service providers where necessary to arrange and provide travel services. This may include airlines, accommodation providers, transport operators and destination partners, including those overseas. Overseas transfers must meet applicable data-protection requirements.

Your choices and requests

You may contact hello@senyumrihlat.com to ask about your personal information, request access or correction, withdraw consent or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Requests are subject to applicable legal exceptions.

If you withdraw consent needed to arrange a trip, this may affect the services we can provide. We will explain the implications. Any booking cancellation remains subject to the booking terms.

The invoice terms include a photography and promotional-use clause. Please contact us with any questions about that clause or the use of your image.

Protection and retention

Personal information should be protected with reasonable safeguards and retained only while needed for the purpose for which it was collected or for legal or business requirements. Senyum must confirm its operational safeguards and retention arrangements before this draft is adopted.

Website services and external links

This preview uses third-party hosting and font services. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page or font. Links to WhatsApp, maps and other external services are subject to those providers’ own privacy policies.

The production website’s cookies, analytics and spam-protection services will be reviewed and this policy updated before launch. Cloudflare Turnstile is planned for the enquiry form after deployment and is not active on this preview.

Contact and updates

For privacy enquiries, contact hello@senyumrihlat.com or call +65 8400 0806. Our address is 80 Changi Road, #02-29 Centropod @ Changi, Singapore 419715. Please indicate that your message concerns personal data.

This policy will be updated when the website or data-handling arrangements change. Draft prepared on 12 November 2026; client confirmation is pending.
