Your time in Jordan.

DAY01

Arrival - Queen Alia International Airport - Amman

+

Airport Arrival. Meet with tour Guide and transfer direct to your hotel. Depending upon your flight arrival time, we can offer additional activities for the day.

Dinner and overnight in Amman.

Meals: Dinner

DAY02

Amman - Amman City tour - Desert Castles - Amman

+

Depart from your hotel and begin your tour of the old city of Amman exploring

The Citadel and the Roman Theatre, two of the most popular archaeological sites in the city. Then walk through the old markets, viewing the Al-Husseini Mosque and stop at a local restaurant to sample authentic Falafel and Hummus. Depart from Amman .Head east into the desert and visit some of Jordan’s Desert Castles. They are beautiful examples of early Islamic art and architecture, with fine mosaics and frescos. See the stunning artwork in the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Qasr Amra. Return to Amman.

Dinner and overnight in Amman.

Meals: Breakfast · Dinner

DAY03

Amman - Jerash - Ajloun - Um Qais - Amman

+

Head north to walk through history in Jerash, regarded as one of the most important and best preserved Roman cities in the Middle East. Then on to Ajloun and visit the castle, dominating the Ajloun skyline and built to ward off the Crusader armies at the key trade and pilgrimage intersection between Jerusalem, Cairo, Damascus and Mecca.

Travel to the northern tip of Jordan and savor the beauty of Umm Qais, the Greek and Roman city overlooking the Jordan valley and the Sea of Galilee.

Dinner and overnight in Amman

Meals: Breakfast · Dinner

DAY04

Amman - Madaba - Mount Nebo - Keark- Petra

+

After breakfast, drive to Mount Nebo and wonder at the view where Moses looked across to the Promised Land and later died and was buried. Then on to the town of Madaba and discover the amazing Byzantine mosaics excavated from throughout the town. See the ‘Madaba Map’, the oldest preserved mosaic map of the Holy Lands.

Continue south to the ancient crusader castle in Karak, exploring the maze of stone- vaulted halls and passageways. The city of Karak was the capital of Moab and the castle dominates the skyline. The castle provides an impressive insight into the architectural and military skills of the Crusaders. Then drive to your hotel in Petra.

Dinner and overnight in Petra.

Meals: Breakfast · Dinner

DAY05

Petra - Petra visit - Petra

+

After breakfast to the red rose city Petra, the biggest attraction of Jordan. Petra was first established around the 6th century B.C. by the Nabataean Arabs, a nomadic tribe who settled in the area and laid the foundation of a commercial empire that extended into Syria. With the guide pass the canyon, the so called Siq, to see the most beautiful monument: the Treasury. Continue via the Street of Facades to the Royal Tombs and Pharaoh’s Castle. After that, free time to explore Petra on your own.

After tour ends, drive back to your hotel.

Dinner and overnight in Petra.

Meals: Breakfast · Dinner

DAY06

Petra - Little Petra - Wadi Rum

+

After breakfast, Then on Al Beidha, better known as Little Petra. Little Petra was an important suburb of Petra, the entry and exit point for the trade routes to the north and north-west, Drive to Wadi Rum and marvel at the sunset and gaze at the stars in the night sky whilst enjoying the legendary hospitality of the Bedouin, before sleeping at the foot of the awesome mountains exploding out from the desert landscape).

Dinner and overnight under the stars in Wadi Rum.

Meals: Breakfast · Dinner

DAY07

Wadi Rum - Dead Sea - Amman

+

Enjoy the wilderness adventure of a 4×4 jeep tour of Wadi Rum, following in the footsteps of Lawrence of Arabia, climbing sand dunes and rock bridges, enjoy the experience of the Camel ride for 2 hours in Wadi Rum before the Depart from Wadi Rum to the Dead Sea to enjoy the rest of your day relaxing, floating in the salt waters, or resting by the pool. You can always pamper yourself with your chosen spa treatment! Return to Amman.

Dinner and overnight in Amman.

Meals: Breakfast · Dinner

DAY08

Departure Queen Alia International Airport

+

After breakfast, transfer to Queen Alia International Airport for departure.

End of tour.

Meals: Breakfast
